THE PROJECT

Theory and computation in one environment.

This course presents the 12 numbered lessons from the 2024 USP graduate course taught by Prof. João Bosco A. London Junior, followed by the separate dynamic-estimation lecture as Lesson 13. The English presentations combine Markdown, LaTeX, original illustrations, worked examples, and study guides.

The laboratories revisit Assignments 2, 3, and 6, the redundancy example from Lesson 8, and teaching extensions defined in the lessons. The final lesson draws on the dynamic-estimation lecture by Dr. Julio A. D. Massignan and Prof. João Bosco A. London Junior. Times shown on lesson cards are suggested study durations.

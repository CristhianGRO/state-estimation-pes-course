4. Guided student challenges

  1. Choose Minimal observable. Why can every channel be individually critical even though the network is observable?
  2. Choose Two dependent. Identify the unobservable direction and explain why meter count equals state count but rank does not.
  3. Enable Pseudo P13 for the dependent case. Does rank change? Discuss why its low weight gives a large uncertainty in θ3.
  4. Switch the restoration to Duplicate P12. Why does rank not increase?
  5. Enable All channels. Find the smallest loss sets and compare single-channel failures with full RTU failures.
Interpretation: this tool tests exact row rank and small-set contingencies for the stated DC model. It does not identify general observable islands with injections, reproduce historical sparse factorization algorithms, or simulate an AC WLS estimator.
